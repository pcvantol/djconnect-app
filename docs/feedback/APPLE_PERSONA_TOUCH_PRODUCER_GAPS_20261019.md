## Observed producer gap: withdrawal after delivery

`persona_reactions.withdraw_for_interaction` increments a RAM-only interaction
revision and disposes pending/held reaction sources after another accepted Ask DJ,
manual speech or configured preview. Its `_Lease` guard also withdraws on provider,
Session, settings or owner changes. These prevent later delivery or byte reads.

However, there is no delivered consumer event/read contract identifying that
original reaction's continuing admission after text/bytes have arrived. The
ordinary operation status is a durable outcome receipt only; `hybrid_service.status`
returns no content and does not retain the RAM-only source admission callbacks.
`transport_delivery.OwnerDeliveryResult` uses those callbacks only for its original
in-flight delivery. A confirmed receipt must therefore NEVER be polled/interpreted
as permission to continue displaying or playing a reaction. Local settings/epoch/
connection checks cannot observe every cross-device Ask DJ or HA provider change.

Apple can stop its owned renderer immediately on locally observed dismissal,
foreground Ask DJ/retry, capture, other speech, background, route/interruption,
Session/owner/settings/connection changes and the original deadline. That does not
prove a stop for an unobservable remote withdrawal. The delivered maximum30seconds
is not a substitute for withdrawal. No heartbeat, guessed Broadcast enum or client
invented new wire field is introduced to hide this gap.

## Observed producer gap: cancellation can race admission

Core's content-free `async_cancel` checks existing pending/lease rows and is
idempotently successful when there is no matching row. It does not retain a
pre-admission cancellation tombstone. A delayed original execute can therefore
arrive after its cancellation and still create a held lease. Apple suppresses all
late local UI/audio and cancels the original identity, but cannot promise that the
server never holds that late lease. Re-executing the reaction is forbidden, and a
new arbitrary timer/cancel intent is not a producer ordering guarantee.
